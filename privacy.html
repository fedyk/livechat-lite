Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how FEDYK sp. z o. o. ("we", "us", and "our"), a member of the LiveChat Developer Program, handles data when you use LiteChat for LiveChat (the "Service"). The Service is a browser-based client for LiveChat agents.

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at support@livechat-apps.com.

2. Our Privacy Principles

  • We do not sell your data or use it to show you advertisements.
  • We do not use analytics tracking.
  • Data saved by the client is stored in your browser, rather than in a user database on our servers.
  • We use Sentry for error reporting in the application, as described below. Sentry is not loaded on our Privacy Policy or Terms of Service pages.

3. Data Used by the Service

The Service accesses your LiveChat account to display and manage chats, messages, attachments, agent information, and related customer information according to your account permissions. This data is exchanged with LiveChat to provide the features you use.

The client uses browser local storage to save your agent profile, authentication credentials (including access and refresh tokens), account identifiers, recent search queries, and interface preferences. Local storage allows the application to retain this information between visits.

Some requests, including authentication, canned responses, and browser information parsing, pass through our service endpoints. We do not retain your account data, tokens, messages, or files on our servers.

4. LiveChat and Sentry

LiveChat processes and stores data as part of its own service. Browser-only storage by our client does not mean that your LiveChat chats and account data exist only on your device. For details, see the LiveChat Privacy Policy.

We use Sentry to receive application error reports and diagnose technical problems. These reports may include error details and diagnostic context. Our application also attaches error data, error types, and response status information when available. This diagnostic information is sent to Sentry and is an exception to the client's browser-only storage of data.

We use error reporting to maintain the reliability of the Service, not for advertising or analytics tracking. Sentry error reporting runs in the application, but not on these legal pages.

5. Purpose of Processing

We process data to provide the Service, authenticate your LiveChat account, carry out your actions, and remember your preferences. We rely on our legitimate interests in providing and maintaining an effective Service when processing diagnostic information to identify and resolve errors.

6. Storage, Security, and Deletion

Information saved in browser local storage remains there until it is replaced or removed. You can remove it by clearing the site's data in your browser settings. This removes locally saved credentials and preferences; you will need to sign in again to use the Service.

Clearing browser data does not delete information held by LiveChat or diagnostic reports already sent to Sentry. Contact us at support@livechat-apps.com with questions or requests about diagnostic data. Manage data held by LiveChat through your LiveChat account and its privacy procedures.

The Service uses HTTPS for its API requests. Because credentials are stored in your browser, protect access to your device and browser profile, particularly when using a shared device.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Please review this page for the latest information about our privacy practices. If we make material changes, we will post a notice on the website before they take effect. If you do not accept the changes, discontinue use of the Service.
